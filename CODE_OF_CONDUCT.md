# Code of conduct

Adaptive Sports Near Me is built by and for the adaptive sports community. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their disability, body, age, background, identity or experience with code.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Use the words people use for themselves.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Make room for people who are new, and for people who communicate differently.
- Remember that access needs are real needs, not preferences.

## What isn't okay

Harassment, insults, slurs, unwanted attention, and jokes or comments about anyone's disability, body or identity. Sharing someone's private information without their yes.

## If something happens

Email hello@adaptivesportsnearme.com. A person at Adapt To Life reads it, keeps it private, and replies. Maintainers may remove comments, code or people that break this code of conduct.

This code of conduct is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1, which also applies here.
