Why I would pick B

  • For most people today, "near me" means their state: almost no listing has an exact location yet. The map gets them there in one tap.
  • The map is the one picture we will never be waiting on, and it gets better on its own as listings fill in.
  • It is honest about coverage, which turns gaps into an invitation: "help us fill in your state."
  • I would keep A's search box and its "in your city, then elsewhere in your state" list on B's state pages.

Whichever you pick, the real build will be pages the server renders, one for every state, sport and listing, so Google can find "adaptive sports near me" in each state. Everything from the last round stays underneath: verified labels, suggest an update, grants, and Karen's veteran business page.
