Sport images

Every sport in the database, most listings first. One image per sport, like your basketball one. When an image lands here, the site uses it on every card, listing page and sport button for that sport.

How to make each one

  • Square, at least 1000 by 1000 pixels. Basketball was 1254.
  • Black on a white or see-through background. One color, no gray.
  • Your logo athlete plus one clear thing from the sport, like the ball.
  • Bold shapes. It shows as small as a fingertip in the sport menu (the small one).
  • Send it in chat with the sport's name. If you save files, name them by the key, like tennis.png.

Counts are listings a person can act on, sorted into one sport each by their sport label. Live is today's public site. Staging is the bigger list being checked before it goes live.
