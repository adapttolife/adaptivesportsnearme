About

Adaptive Sports Near Me

The project

A free, open, national directory of adaptive sports programs. No logins, no walls. Built and run by Adapt To Life, a recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so every athlete can find their place to play.

How we verify listings

Listings come from public directories and community submissions. Automated checks re-visit each program's website on a schedule, and every proposed change is reviewed by a person before it goes live. Badges on a listing tell you exactly what has and hasn't been confirmed.

Accessibility

This site is built for keyboard, screen-reader, and low-vision use, and we hold ourselves to WCAG 2.1 AA as we grow. If anything here is hard to use, tell us through "Add a program → Anything else" and we will fix it.
